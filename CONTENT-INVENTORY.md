## The main finding

Every dimension has the same two kinds of text:

1. **A lesson transcript (.docx, dated 2025-07-08, "May 20, 2025" in the header).** Eleven of these are titled *"Lifebook Lesson N - Category N ..."*. They are **word-for-word, timestamped transcripts of Jon Butcher's Mindvalley *Lifebook* video lessons**. They name "Lifebook", "the LifeBook community", Missy (Jon's wife), Jon's own premise/vision statements and his family stories, and one mentions "the Mind Valley communities". Each runs about 9–11k words, roughly 1h10m–1h35m of video. They are source/reference material. They can't be used as LifeCharter content without a full rewrite.
2. **A LifeCharter workbook (PDF, Aug 2024, about 45–60 MB, around 70 pages).** It is branded "Realization Academy", "Sacred Kaleidoscope Community" and "©2023 AmiLynne Carroll". Every workbook follows the same Premise / Vision / Purpose / Strategy template, with "Babs & Member Wisdom" example statements, quotes and ruled note pages. Only the Life Vision PDF (and the Emotional Life workbook, via its Google Doc copy) returned text. The other 10 PDFs came back empty from the Drive text extractor. They are most likely image or outlined-text exports, so their contents are inferred from the shared template.

Health & Fitness is the exception. Its transcript (`Health & Fitness Dimension.docx`, Oct 2024) is **an earlier attempt to adapt the Lifebook Lesson 1 transcript**: "Lifebook" became "LifeCharter", "category" became "dimension", "LifeCharter members worldwide" was used, a few of Babs's own lines were added (for example "yes, even as a quadriplegic"), and "5 MINUTE BREAK TO COMPLETE … WORK" markers were inserted. The paragraph flow, premise-belief framing, anecdotes and "member" statements still follow Jon Butcher's lesson closely.

There are **no video or audio files anywhere in the tree.** The workbooks point to "NOTES FROM AUDIO/VIDEO LESSON", so the lesson media lives somewhere else or hasn't been made yet.
