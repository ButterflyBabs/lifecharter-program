# LifeCharter Program — Build Brief

Starting context for the LifeCharter Program build thread (written 2026-09-27).

## What it is
The core program on the LifeCharter path: **Incubator → The Life Shift (21-Day Challenge) → LifeCharter Program.**
For people ready to live on purpose: to find clarity, heal what's holding them back, and design a balanced, authentic life across all 12 dimensions.

LifeCharter is **not** LifeCharter Command Suite (the business/founder product at lccommandsuite.com). Keep them separate.

## Model to mirror
Mindvalley's Lifebook Online: https://home.mindvalley.com/quests/en/lifebook-online
Mirror the *experience and structure* (a guided online program where members work through life categories, define premise/vision/purpose/strategy for each, and build a personal life book). Write all content in Babs's own voice and framework; don't copy Mindvalley's text, images or branding.
