## 1. Welcome, and closing the Circle

Welcome back. This week we close the Circle.

We've looked at your love relationship and at the young lives you help grow. Now we widen out to your friendships, your community, and the people you choose to travel with. This is **Social Life**.

You know my title is Chief Travel Partner. That's not an accident. I believe no one should have to travel their path alone, and this week is about who travels yours with you.

## 2. Monarchs don't winter alone

Here's the image for this week.

When the cold comes, monarch butterflies gather by the thousands, sometimes millions, on the same trees. They cluster together, wing against wing, and help each other make it through the cold season.

Even the most beautiful wings need company to make it through winter.

We were never meant to do life alone, either. Research on happiness, health and even how long we live keeps landing in the same place: the quality of our relationships matters enormously.

## 3. Who this week is for

A quick word before we begin.

If you're an introvert, this week isn't about filling your calendar or becoming the life of the party. It's about **depth**: a few real connections that feed you.

And if you feel lonely right now, please hear me: loneliness is very common, and it isn't a flaw in you. Seasons of life, moves, divorce, loss, illness, disability, caregiving and busy years with small children can all shrink our circles. Circles can grow again.

## 4. Movement 1: The Air I'm In

Let's look honestly at your social life today.

We'll check in on six parts of it:

- **Close friendships**: people who really know me
- **Time together**: how often I actually see or talk with them
- **Belonging**: a community or group where I feel I'm part of something
- **Showing up for others**: how well I'm a friend
- **Fun and play**: laughter, adventure, lightness
- **Letting people in**: whether I let myself be known, and helped

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: The Air I'm In**
> 1. On a scale of 1 to 10, my social life today is a ___.
> 2. Rate each of the six parts from 1 to 10.
> 3. Three words that describe my social life right now…
> 4. The people I'd call if everything fell apart…
> 5. Where I feel lonely or left out…

## 5. Movement 2: My Truth

Our beliefs about friendship often come from the playground, from the family we grew up in, or from being hurt by someone we trusted.

See which of these feel familiar:

- "I'm too busy for friends right now."
- "People don't really want me around."
- "Making friends as an adult is impossible."
- "I have to be the strong one. I don't need anyone."
- "It's worth reaching out first."
- "I can be a good friend and still have boundaries."

Same three questions: **Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?**

Then write **My Truth**: what you choose to believe about friendship, community and belonging.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Truth**
> 1. List the beliefs you carry about friendship and community.
> 2. Mark each one: **mine** or **handed to me**, **lifts** or **weighs**.
> 3. Write **My Truth**: three to five sentences, present tense.

## 6. Movement 3: My Horizon

Now picture your social life thriving.

Who's around your table? What do you do together? How often do you laugh? Who do you call on a hard day, and who calls you? Where do you belong: a faith community, a group, a team, a circle of women, a club? How do you contribute to the people and places you love?

Then one more question: **What kind of friend am I becoming?** Who are you to the people in your circle?

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Horizon**
> 1. Describe my social life thriving, in the present tense.
> 2. The kind of friend I'm becoming:

## 7. Movement 4: My Why

Why does this matter to you?

Good friends make the joys bigger and the burdens lighter. They tell us the truth. They remind us who we are when we forget. And the friend you are ripples out into other people's lives in ways you'll never fully see.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Why**
> 1. A thriving social life matters to me because…
> 2. The people who will feel the difference are…
> 3. If nothing changes here, what it costs me is…

## 8. Movement 5: My Flight Plan

Your Flight Plan this week has four parts. Wing-sized.

- **Nurture**: the two or three friendships you'll invest in on purpose. A standing call, a monthly lunch, a walk-and-talk or roll-and-talk, a weekly text thread.
- **Reach out**: one new connection, or one old friendship you'd like to rekindle. Who could you invite?
- **Belong**: a community where you can be known over time. A faith community, a class, a volunteer team, a support group, or the LifeCharter Collective, where your fellow travelers already are.
- **Boundaries**: the relationships that drain you, and what you'll protect so your energy goes to the people who feed you.

## 9. Movement 6: My Next Right Movement

One thing, this week.

Send the text. Make the call. Put the lunch on the calendar. Say yes to the invitation. Introduce yourself in the Collective.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Next Right Movement**
> This week, I will… (what, when, and how I'll know I did it)

## 10. Movement 7: Soul Challenge: "Seven Reaches"

Your Soul Challenge this week is called **Seven Reaches**.

Each day for seven days, reach toward one person, on purpose. A text that says "I was thinking about you." A call. An invitation. A thank-you note. A check-in with someone who's going through something hard. A hello to someone new.

Write one line each day: who you reached toward, and what happened.

Some reaches will come back right away. Some won't. That's okay. You're practicing being the kind of person who reaches.

## 11. Closing

Here's what you did this week. You looked honestly at your circle. You chose what you believe about friendship and belonging. You pictured your social life thriving and named the friend you're becoming. And you built a plan to nurture, reach out and belong.

That's the eighth chapter of your Charter, and it closes the Circle.

Bring your Seven Reaches notes to this week's Gathering. Who reached back, and what surprised you?
