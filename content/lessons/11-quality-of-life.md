## 1. Welcome to the last week of the Flight

Welcome back. This is our last week in the Flight, and our last dimension before Wings Out.

We've looked at the fuel of your finances and the work of your career. Now we ask a simple question that turns out to be very big: **how do your days actually feel?**

This is **Quality of Life**: the joy, beauty, rest, play and adventure that make a life not just productive, but worth living.

## 2. Butterflies bask

Here's the image for this week.

Have you ever seen a butterfly resting on a sunny rock, wings spread wide, not moving? It's not being lazy. Butterflies need the sun's warmth to power their flight muscles. On a cool morning, a butterfly *can't* fly until it has basked.

Rest, warmth and delight aren't a reward you earn after the flight. **They're what make the flight possible.**

So many of us have it backwards. We tell ourselves we'll enjoy life once the work is done, once the kids are grown, once things settle down. But things rarely settle down. The days pass while we're waiting to live them.

## 3. Movement 1: The Air I'm In

Let's look honestly at how your days feel right now.

We'll check in on six parts of your quality of life:

- **Joy and delight**: how often I feel glad to be alive
- **Rest and renewal**: real rest, not just collapsing
- **Beauty around me**: my home and surroundings, and how they feel to be in
- **Play and creativity**: time to make, play and be lighthearted
- **Adventure and new experiences**: something to look forward to
- **Time for what I love**: whether my days include the things I love

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: The Air I'm In**
> 1. On a scale of 1 to 10, my quality of life today is a ___.
> 2. Rate each of the six parts from 1 to 10.
> 3. Three words that describe how my days feel…
> 4. An ordinary day in my life right now looks like…
> 5. What steals joy from my days…

## 4. Movement 2: My Truth

Our beliefs about joy and rest often come from a culture that treats busyness as a badge of honor, or from families where fun was a luxury no one could afford.

See which of these feel familiar:

- "Joy is a reward I have to earn first."
- "Rest is lazy."
- "I'll enjoy life once things settle down."
- "The good things in life need lots of money, or perfect health."
- "Small joys, noticed, make a big life."
- "Rest makes flight possible."

Same three questions: **Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?**

Then write **My Truth**: what you choose to believe about joy, rest and a life well lived.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Truth**
> 1. List the beliefs you carry about joy, rest and enjoying life.
> 2. Mark each one: **mine** or **handed to me**, **lifts** or **weighs**.
> 3. Write **My Truth**: three to five sentences, present tense.

## 5. Movement 3: My Horizon

Now let's picture your quality of life thriving, and I want you to picture something specific: not a vacation, but **an ideal ordinary day**.

A regular day, when your life is the way you want it. How do you wake up? What's the first thing you see? What do you eat, and with whom? What fills your morning, your afternoon, your evening? Where is the beauty? Where is the laughter? How do you rest? How do you feel when you close your eyes that night?

Then name a few **experiences you want to have**, big and small. A trip. A concert. A garden. Learning to paint. Watching a sunrise at the ocean. Dinner with everyone you love around one table.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Horizon**
> 1. My ideal ordinary day, from morning to night, in the present tense.
> 2. Experiences I want to have, big and small:

## 6. Movement 4: My Why

Why does this matter to you?

Your quality of life is what all the other dimensions are *for*. Health, money, work and relationships matter partly because they let you live days that feel good to live. And a person who knows how to rest and delight brings that lightness to everyone around them.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Why**
> 1. Days that feel good to live matter to me because…
> 2. The people who will feel the difference are…
> 3. If nothing changes here, what it costs me is…

## 7. Movement 5: My Flight Plan

Your Flight Plan this week has four parts. Wing-sized, and joyful, I hope.

- **Daily joy**: one small delight built into every day. Your favorite music in the morning. Tea on the porch. Ten minutes with a book. Time with Beau, or your own four-legged friend.
- **Rest and renewal**: your rhythm of real rest. A true day off each week. Breaks in the workday. A bedtime you protect. Time away each year.
- **My space**: one change that makes your surroundings more beautiful, peaceful or accessible. Clear one surface. Add a plant. Bring in light. Make the room work for your body.
- **Adventure**: one experience on the calendar, big or small, so there's always something to look forward to.

## 9. Movement 7: Soul Challenge: "Bask"

Your Soul Challenge this week is called **Bask**.

Each day for seven days, give yourself ten minutes to bask. Savor something good, with nothing else competing for your attention. No phone, no multitasking. The sun on your face. A piece of music, start to finish. A meal, slowly. A child's laugh. The first sip of coffee.

Write one line each day: what you basked in, and how it felt.

You might be surprised how much more of your life you notice when you give yourself permission to stop and warm your wings.

## 10. Closing

Here's what you did this week. You looked honestly at how your days feel. You chose what you believe about joy and rest. You pictured your ideal ordinary day and named the experiences you want to have. And you built a plan for joy, rest, beauty and adventure.

That's the eleventh chapter of your Charter, and it completes the Flight.

Bring your Bask notes to this week's Gathering. What did you find when you slowed down to savor?

Next week is **Wings Out**. We bring all eleven dimensions together into your **Life Vision**, and then you'll **sign your LifeCharter**. Please bring every chapter page you've written. We're going to put them all together.

Head up. Wings out.
