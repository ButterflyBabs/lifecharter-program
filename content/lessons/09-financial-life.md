## 1. Welcome to the Flight

Welcome to **The Flight**.

We've done the inside work in the Cocoon, cared for body and mind in the Vessel, and tended our people in the Circle. For the next three weeks we look at how you're provided for, how you spend your working hours, and how your days actually feel: your financial life, your career, and your quality of life.

We begin with **Financial Life**.

I know money can be one of the most loaded words in the language. For some of you, your shoulders just went up around your ears. That's okay. Take a breath. We'll go gently, and we'll go honestly.

## 2. A note before we begin

This lesson is for reflection and planning. It isn't financial, legal or tax advice, and I'm not a financial advisor. For decisions about investments, debt, taxes or benefits, please talk with a qualified professional. If money stress feels overwhelming, a nonprofit credit counselor can be a good place to start.

## 3. Fuel for the flight

Here's the image for this week.

Before their long journey, monarch butterflies spend their days feeding on flower nectar, building up the reserves that will carry them thousands of miles. They don't hoard, and they don't starve. They gather what they need for the flight ahead.

In LifeCharter, money is **fuel for the flight**. It isn't a measure of your worth, and it isn't the point of your life. It's a tool, a way of turning what you value into what you actually do.

Your financial life thriving doesn't mean being rich. It means **enough**, **clarity**, and **peace**: knowing where you stand, having a plan, and using money to serve what you love.

## 4. Movement 1: The Air I'm In

Let's look honestly at your financial life today. And let's do it without shame. Shame keeps us from looking, and we can't change what we won't look at.

We'll check in on six parts of your financial life:

- **Knowing my numbers**: what comes in, what goes out, what I owe, what I have
- **Spending on purpose**: does my spending match what matters to me?
- **Cushion**: savings for emergencies and the unexpected
- **Handling debt**: a plan I'm working, or a weight I avoid
- **Giving**: generosity in whatever form I can offer
- **Peace about money**: how much money worry I carry

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: The Air I'm In**
> 1. On a scale of 1 to 10, my financial life today is a ___.
> 2. Rate each of the six parts from 1 to 10.
> 3. Three words that describe my relationship with money…
> 4. How I feel when I check my balance…
> 5. The money story I grew up with…

## 5. Movement 2: My Truth

Few beliefs are handed to us more quietly, or held more deeply, than our beliefs about money. We learned them by watching who worried, who fought, who went without, and who never talked about it at all.

See which of these feel familiar:

- "I'm just bad with money."
- "There will never be enough."
- "Wanting more money makes me greedy."
- "If I don't look, it isn't real."
- "Money is a tool that can serve what I love."
- "I can learn to handle money well, one small step at a time."

Same three questions: **Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?**

Then write **My Truth**: what you choose to believe about money and about yourself with money.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Truth**
> 1. List the beliefs you carry about money.
> 2. Mark each one: **mine** or **handed to me**, **lifts** or **weighs**.
> 3. Write **My Truth**: three to five sentences, present tense.

## 6. Movement 3: My Horizon

Now picture your financial life thriving.

Not a lottery-win fantasy. A real, grounded picture. How do you feel when you check your accounts? What bills are paid, and what worries are gone? What cushion do you have? What debt have you cleared? How do you give? What conversations about money have become easy in your home?

Then one more question: **What do I want my money to make possible?** Security for my family? Freedom to rest? Generosity? Experiences with the people I love? Something to leave behind? This is where money connects back to everything else in your Charter.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Horizon**
> 1. Describe my financial life thriving, in the present tense.
> 2. What I want my money to make possible:

## 7. Movement 4: My Why

Why does this matter to you?

Money stress touches everything: sleep, health, relationships, even how we pray. Clarity and peace with money free up energy for everything else in your Charter. And a well-tended financial life lets you care for the people and causes you love.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Why**
> 1. Peace with money matters to me because…
> 2. The people who will feel the difference are…
> 3. If nothing changes here, what it costs me is…

## 8. Movement 5: My Flight Plan

Your Flight Plan this week has four parts. Remember: wing-sized, starting from where you really are.

- **Know my numbers**: a regular money date with yourself, or with your partner. Thirty minutes, once a week or once a month, to look at what came in, what went out, and what's coming.
- **Spend on purpose**: a simple plan that sends your money toward what matters most, and away from what doesn't.
- **Cushion and debt**: one step. Starting an emergency fund, even a small one. Listing every debt in one place. Choosing which one to tackle first.
- **Generosity and support**: how you'll give, in money, time or skill. And who helps you: a financial professional, a credit counselor, a class, or an accountability partner.

## 9. Movement 6: My Next Right Movement

One thing, this week.

Log in and look. List your debts in one place. Set up an automatic transfer of even five dollars to savings. Book your first money date. Cancel the subscription you forgot about.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Next Right Movement**
> This week, I will… (what, when, and how I'll know I did it)

## 10. Movement 7: Soul Challenge: "Follow the Flow"

Your Soul Challenge this week is called **Follow the Flow**.

Each day for seven days, notice one money moment: a purchase, a bill, a gift, a moment of worry, or a moment of enough. Write one line: what it was, whether it matched what matters to you, and how it felt.

Add one thank-you each day for something that provided for you, whether it was a paycheck, a meal, a gift or a kindness.

No judgment. You're just following where your money flows, and what it tells you about what you value.

## 11. Closing

Here's what you did this week. You looked at your finances honestly, without shame. You chose what you believe about money. You pictured your financial life thriving and named what you want your money to make possible. And you built a plan, starting from where you really are.

That's the ninth chapter of your Charter.

Bring your Follow the Flow notes to this week's Gathering. What did your money tell you about what you value?

Next week, we stay in the Flight with your **Career**: your work, your calling, and how you spend your working hours.

Head up. Wings out.
