## 1. Welcome back

Welcome back to the Cocoon.

Last week you looked at your connection to whatever is larger than you. This week we turn to the person who carries that connection into every room: **you**. This week is **Character**.

Character is who you are when no one's watching. It's also who you're becoming, one choice at a time.

Remember what "head up" means: remembering who I am before my circumstances try to tell me who I am. This is the week you get to decide, in your own words, who that is.

## 2. What we mean by character

Your character isn't your personality. Personality is whether you're the life of the party or the one in the corner having the best conversation. Character is what you do with who you are.

It's how you treat the person who can do nothing for you. It's what you do when you've made a mistake and no one else knows yet. It's whether your yes means yes. It's how you carry yourself when life hands you a damaged wing.

And here's the good news: character isn't fixed. It isn't something you either have or you don't. It's built, and rebuilt, in small choices, most of them unseen. That means you can grow it at any age, from any starting point.

[BABS: the moment that gave you the name "Bad Ass Butterfly". It's character in action: choosing to act when others stood by. Share what it taught you about who you are, in whatever detail you want this room to have.]

That moment didn't come out of nowhere. Moments like that are built long before they arrive, in a thousand ordinary choices about who we'll be.

## 3. Movement 1: The Air I'm In

Let's start with honest sight, and I want to invite a lot of gentleness here. Looking at our own character can bring up shame fast. That's not what this is for. Clear sight, not self-punishment.

Ask yourself: how closely do my everyday actions match what I say matters to me? Where do I show up as my best self? And where do I slip: with certain people, under certain pressures, when I'm tired or scared?

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: The Air I'm In**
> 1. On a scale of 1 to 10, how well my daily actions match who I want to be: ___
> 2. Three words the people closest to me would use to describe me are…
> 3. Where do I show up as my best self?
> 4. Where, or with whom, do I slip?

## 4. Movement 2: My Truth

Now the beliefs. We all carry beliefs about character: our own, and what character even is. Some of them lift us. Some of them keep us small or stuck. Some were handed to us by a parent, a teacher, a hard experience, or a culture that rewarded looking good over being good.

See which of these feel familiar:

- "I am who I am. People don't really change."
- "Being kind means being a pushover."
- "If I make a mistake, it proves I'm not a good person."
- "My worth depends on what other people think of me."
- "Who I am is built in small, unseen choices."
- "I can grow at any age, from any starting point."

Same three questions as last week, for each belief you carry: **Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?**

Then write **My Truth**: what you choose to believe about your character, and about your ability to grow it.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Truth**
> 1. List the beliefs you carry about character, including your own.
> 2. Mark each one: **mine** or **handed to me**, **lifts** or **weighs**.
> 3. Write **My Truth**: three to five sentences, present tense.

## 5. Movement 3: My Horizon

Now let's picture the person you're becoming.

Start by choosing the qualities you most want to live by. Not the ones you think you *should* choose: the ones that make something in you say *yes, that's me at my best.* In the handout there's a list of words to spark ideas, like honest, courageous, generous, patient, loyal, joyful, humble and fair. Use them, or bring your own. Pick no more than five. A short list you can remember beats a long one you can't.

Then describe yourself living those qualities, in the present tense. How do you handle conflict? How do you treat people when you're under pressure? What do you do when you get it wrong? What's it like to be around you?

[BABS: one or two of your own core qualities, and what one of them looks like on an ordinary day, for example with Beau, or on a hard health day. Only what you want to share.]

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Horizon**
> 1. Circle or write up to five qualities I choose to live by.
> 2. Describe me at my best, present tense: in conflict, under pressure, when I get it wrong, when no one's watching.

## 6. Movement 4: My Why

Why does this matter to you?

Your character is the one thing you take into every dimension of your life. It shapes your marriage and your friendships, how your children see the world, how people experience working with you, how you feel when you lay your head down at night.

When it's hard to choose the better thing, and some days it will be, your why is what helps you choose it anyway.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Why**
> 1. Living these qualities matters to me because…
> 2. The people who will feel the difference are…
> 3. If nothing changes here, what it costs me is…

## 7. Movement 5: My Flight Plan

Character grows through practice, not willpower. So your Flight Plan is about building the practices that make your chosen qualities more natural each day.

Here are four kinds of practices to consider:

- **A daily check-in**: two minutes at the end of the day. Where did I live my qualities today? Where did I miss? What will I do differently tomorrow?
- **A "when this happens" plan**: pick the moment you most often slip, and decide ahead of time what you'll do instead. "When I feel criticized, I'll take one breath before I answer."
- **A repair practice**: how you'll make it right when you get it wrong. Character isn't never failing. It's how quickly and honestly you repair.
- **Someone who helps you see**: one trusted person who can tell you the truth, kindly.

Keep it wing-sized. One practice you'll keep beats four you'll drop.

> 2. My "when this happens" plan: When ___ happens, I will ___.
> 3. How I'll repair when I get it wrong:
> 4. Who helps me see myself clearly:

## 8. Movement 6: My Next Right Movement

One thing, this week. Small enough that you'll do it, specific enough that you'll know you did.

It might be the apology you've been putting off. It might be starting your two-minute check-in tonight. It might be keeping one promise you made to yourself.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Next Right Movement**
> This week, I will… (what, when, and how I'll know I did it)

## 9. Movement 7: Soul Challenge: "The Unseen Choice"

Your Soul Challenge this week is called **The Unseen Choice**.

Each day for seven days, make one small choice that lives out one of your chosen qualities, when no one is watching and no one will ever know. Return the extra change. Speak kindly about someone who isn't in the room. Do the thing you said you'd do, even though no one would notice if you didn't.

Write one line each day: the quality, and the choice.

At the end of the week, read your seven lines. That's your character, being built in real time.

## 10. Closing

Here's what you did this week. You looked honestly, and gently, at the person you are. You chose what you believe about who you can become. You named your qualities and pictured yourself living them. And you built practices to grow into them.

That's the second chapter of your Charter.

Bring your Unseen Choice notes to this week's Gathering. I'd love to hear which choice surprised you.

Next week, we finish the Cocoon with your **Emotional Life**: how you feel, what you do with what you feel, and how to heal what's been holding you back.

Head up. Wings out.
