## 1. Welcome back to the Flight

Welcome back to the Flight.

Last week we looked at the fuel: your financial life. This week we look at where so many of your waking hours go, your work. This is **Career**.

In LifeCharter, "career" is bigger than a job title. It's **your work and your contribution**: what you give your time, skill and energy to. Maybe that's a paycheck job. Maybe it's a business you're building. Maybe it's raising a family, caring for a loved one, volunteering, studying, or creating. Maybe you're between jobs, retired, or your health has changed what work can look like.

All of it counts. You're here.

## 2. The sun as your compass

Here's the image for this week.

Monarch butterflies navigate thousands of miles using the sun as a compass. They adjust through the day as the sun moves, and they keep their bearing toward a place most of them have never seen.

Your **calling** is like that sun. It's the direction that keeps you oriented, even when the path twists and the job titles change. Your career is the flight. Your calling is the compass.

When your work lines up with your calling, even hard days have meaning. When it doesn't, even easy days can feel empty.

## 3. Movement 1: The Air I'm In

Let's look honestly at your work life today.

We'll check in on six parts of it:

- **Meaning**: does my work matter to me?
- **Using my strengths**: do I get to do what I'm good at?
- **Growth**: am I learning and developing?
- **Workload and energy**: is the pace sustainable?
- **Relationships at work**: the people I work with or serve
- **Fair reward**: pay, recognition, or the value I receive

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: The Air I'm In**
> 1. On a scale of 1 to 10, my work life today is a ___.
> 2. Rate each of the six parts from 1 to 10.
> 3. Three words that describe my work life right now…
> 4. The parts of my work that light me up…
> 5. The parts that drain me…

## 4. Movement 2: My Truth

Our beliefs about work often come from the people who raised us: how hard they worked, what they said about their jobs at the dinner table, what they told us success looks like.

See which of these feel familiar:

- "Work is supposed to be hard. Enjoying it is a luxury."
- "It's too late for me to change direction."
- "My worth is measured by how productive I am."
- "I have to choose between meaningful work and paying the bills."
- "My work can be an expression of who I am."
- "Every season of work can teach me something."

Same three questions: **Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?**

Then write **My Truth**: what you choose to believe about work, calling and your worth.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Truth**
> 1. List the beliefs you carry about work and success.
> 2. Mark each one: **mine** or **handed to me**, **lifts** or **weighs**.
> 3. Write **My Truth**: three to five sentences, present tense.

## 5. Movement 3: My Horizon

Now picture your work life thriving.

What are you doing? Who are you doing it with, or for? Which strengths are you using? What does a good workday feel like, start to finish? How much do you work, and how much do you rest? What are you known for? What are you being paid, or given, in return?

Then one more question: **What difference do I want my work to make?** In one person's life, in your community, or in the world. This is where your calling comes into focus.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Horizon**
> 1. Describe my work life thriving, in the present tense.
> 2. The difference I want my work to make:

## 6. Movement 4: My Why

Why does this matter to you?

Most of us will spend a huge share of our waking lives working. Work that fits your calling gives you energy for everything else in your Charter. Work that doesn't can quietly drain it all.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Why**
> 1. Work aligned with my calling matters to me because…
> 2. The people who will feel the difference are…
> 3. If nothing changes here, what it costs me is…

## 7. Movement 5: My Flight Plan

Your Flight Plan this week has four parts. Wing-sized. You don't have to quit your job on Monday.

- **Strengths**: how you'll use more of what you're good at and love doing, inside your current work or alongside it.
- **Growth**: one skill, course, credential or experience that moves you toward your Horizon.
- **Boundaries and energy**: the hours, rest and "no"s that make your pace sustainable.
- **Allies and next steps**: who can help you: a mentor, a manager, a coach, a network, a colleague who believes in you. And the next conversation you need to have.

## 10. Movement 7: Soul Challenge: "Lift and Drag"

Your Soul Challenge this week is called **Lift and Drag**. Pilots use those words: lift carries you up, drag holds you back.

Each workday for seven days, notice **one task that gave you lift**, meaning it energized you, and **one that created drag**, meaning it drained you. If you're not in paid work, use whatever you spent your day giving your energy to.

Write one line each day: lift, and drag.

At the end of the week, look at the patterns. They'll tell you a lot about your strengths, and about what to change.

## 11. Closing

Here's what you did this week. You looked honestly at your work life. You chose what you believe about work and worth. You pictured your work thriving and named the difference you want to make. And you built a plan that moves toward your calling, one wing-sized step at a time.

That's the tenth chapter of your Charter.

Bring your Lift and Drag notes to this week's Gathering. What gave you lift, and what created drag?

Next week is our last week in the Flight: your **Quality of Life**, meaning how your days actually feel, and the joy, beauty and rest that make a life worth living.

Head up. Wings out.
