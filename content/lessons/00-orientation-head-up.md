## 1. Welcome

Hello, and welcome. I'm so glad you're here.

I'm AmiLynne Carroll. Most people call me Babs. I'm your Chief Travel Partner for the next thirteen weeks, and I mean that title. I won't be up ahead of you on some mountaintop, calling down directions. I'm right here on the path with you, and I'll travel beside you every week.

Let me tell you what we're doing together.

Over the next thirteen weeks you're going to write your **Life Charter**. It's a living document that says who you are, what you believe, what you're reaching for, and how you intend to live across the twelve dimensions of your life. At the end of our time together you'll sign it. Once a year after that, you'll come back and renew it.

This isn't a vision board you'll forget in a drawer. It's a charter: a founding document for the life you're choosing on purpose.

Before we talk about the twelve dimensions, I want to tell you where this program came from, because it came from my own life.

## 2. Head Up, Wings Out

A butterfly with a damaged wing can keep flying. That's not because gravity stops applying to it. It's because the butterfly adapts to the air it's in. It finds the lift that's actually there, and it **renegotiates with the air**.

That's what I've been doing for years now. And it's what this program will teach you to do in your own life.

That's where my motto comes from: **Head up. Wings out.**

**Head up** is about orientation, not pretending. It means remembering who I am before my circumstances try to tell me who I am.

**Wings out** is about willingness, not frantic effort. It means finding the lift that's available in this moment. More glide, less flapping.

I don't need a perfect wing. I don't need a windless day. I don't even need the whole flight plan. I need enough clarity to see the next right movement, and enough courage to extend my wings into it.

I don't write from the mountaintop. I write from the path. And every lesson in this program comes from that same place.

## 3. What a Life Charter is

The whole program rests on three words: **Purpose, Clarity, Aligned Action.**

- **Purpose** is your orientation: head up. Who you are and what matters.
- **Clarity** is honest sight of the air you're actually in, not the air you wish you were in.
- **Aligned Action** is wings out: the next right movement, taken on purpose.

We'll bring those three words into each of the twelve dimensions of your life:

Spiritual Life. Character. Emotional Life. Health & Fitness. Intellectual Life. Love Relationship. Parenting. Social Life. Financial Life. Career. Quality of Life. And Life Vision, which brings them all together.

Some of these will light you up. One or two may make your stomach drop a little. That's okay, and it's actually useful information. We'll talk about it in a minute.

## 4. How our thirteen weeks are shaped

We take **one dimension a week**. Not two, not a rush. One. I built it this way on purpose, because real change needs room to breathe.

The dimensions are grouped in the order a butterfly becomes:

- **The Cocoon** (Weeks 1–3): Spiritual Life, Character, Emotional Life. We start on the inside, with the healing and the knowing.
- **The Vessel** (Weeks 4–5): Health & Fitness, Intellectual Life. Your body and your mind, exactly as they are today.
- **The Circle** (Weeks 6–8): Love Relationship, Parenting, Social Life. Your people.
- **The Flight** (Weeks 9–11): Financial Life, Career, Quality of Life. Provision, work, and how your days actually feel.
- **Wings Out** (Week 12): Life Vision. We bring everything together, and you sign your Charter.

Every week follows the same rhythm:

1. **A lesson** like this one. Watch it when it suits you.
2. **Your Charter pages** for the week, in the app or the workbook.
3. **A Soul Challenge**, one small practice to live the week, not just think it.
4. **The live Weekly LifeCharter Gathering** with me and your fellow travelers.
5. **The Collective**, where you can share, ask and cheer each other on between Gatherings.

## 5. The seven movements of each dimension

Inside every dimension, you'll move through the same seven movements. Learn them once and they'll carry you all the way through.

1. **The Air I'm In.** An honest look at where this part of your life is today. No judgment, just clear sight.
2. **My Truth.** What you actually believe about this part of your life. Some of those beliefs lift you. Some quietly hold you down. Some aren't even yours: they were handed to you. You get to decide which ones stay.
3. **My Horizon.** What this part of your life looks like when it's thriving. Written in the present tense, as if it's already here.
4. **My Why.** Why that horizon matters to you. Your reasons are the fuel. When motivation runs out, your why is still there.
5. **My Flight Plan.** How you'll get there: the practices, choices and boundaries that make your horizon real.
6. **My Next Right Movement.** One action for this week. Not the whole plan. Just the next right movement.
7. **Soul Challenge.** A practice to live the dimension out in real life.

Your Truth, Horizon, Why and Flight Plan are the heart of your Charter. They're what you'll sign in Week 12.

## 6. Your first pause: the air you're in

Now we'll do the first piece of real work together: a snapshot of the air you're in right now, across all twelve dimensions.

You'll rate each dimension from 1 to 10. A 1 means "this part of my life hurts or feels empty." A 10 means "this part of my life is thriving exactly as I'd want."

Please be honest here, not harsh. Honest. This isn't a grade, it's a starting point. And we'll do it again in Week 12, so you'll be able to see how far you've traveled.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: My Starting Snapshot**
> 1. Rate each of the 12 dimensions from 1 to 10.
> 2. Circle the one that feels most alive right now. Write one sentence on why.
> 3. Circle the one that feels heaviest. Write one sentence on why.

Welcome back.

Whatever you circled as heaviest, please hear me: that number isn't who you are. It's just the air you're in today, and air can change.

## 7. Your second pause: why now

Something brought you here, now, to this program. I'd love for you to name it.

> **⏸ PAUSE & WRITE: Why I'm Here**
> 1. What made me say yes to this program now?
> 2. If these thirteen weeks go beautifully, what will be different in me by the end?
> 3. What might get in my way, and what will I do when it shows up?

## 8. How to travel this well

A few agreements before we begin.

**You can't do this wrong.** Some weeks you'll write pages. Some weeks you'll write three lines. Both count.

**Keep a pace you can sustain.** If a week gets away from you, don't try to catch up on everything at once. Do the next right movement and keep going. The lessons will wait for you.

**Some dimensions won't fit your life right now,** maybe Parenting or Love Relationship. You'll still find something in those weeks. We'll talk about how when we get there.

**Tell the truth.** Your Charter is private unless you choose to share it. Write it for you.

**Come to the Gatherings.** Live if you can, the replay if you can't. Something happens when we do this together that just doesn't happen alone.
