Movement 2

My Truth

Look at each belief you carry about this part of your life. Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?

Beliefs people sometimes carry (which feel familiar?)

  • If it's right, it should just be easy.
  • I have to lose myself to keep someone.
  • It's too late for me to find love, or to rebuild it.
  • Conflict means we're failing.
  • Love is a choice I make every day.
  • I deserve to be loved well, and I can learn to love well.
