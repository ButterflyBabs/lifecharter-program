Movement 2

My Truth

Look at each belief you carry about this part of your life. Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?

Beliefs people sometimes carry (which feel familiar?)

  • Joy is a reward I have to earn first.
  • Rest is lazy.
  • I'll enjoy life once things settle down.
  • The good things in life need lots of money, or perfect health.
  • Small joys, noticed, make a big life.
  • Rest makes flight possible.
