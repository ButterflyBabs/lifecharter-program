Your path

Thirteen weeks, one dimension at a time

Purpose is head up. Clarity is honest sight of the air you're in. Aligned Action is wings out. We bring all three into each dimension of your life.

Weeks 1–3The CocoonSpiritual Life, Character, Emotional Life. The inside first.
Weeks 4–5The VesselHealth & Fitness, Intellectual Life. Body and mind.
Weeks 6–8The CircleLove Relationship, Parenting, Social Life. Your people.
Weeks 9–11The FlightFinancial Life, Career, Quality of Life.
Week 12Wings OutLife Vision, then you sign your Charter.

Every week

The seven movements

1. The Air I'm In
An honest look at where this part of life is today.
2. My Truth
The beliefs I choose to live by.
3. My Horizon
This part of life thriving, in the present tense.
4. My Why
Why it matters: the lift under my wings.
5. My Flight Plan
Wing-sized practices, choices and boundaries.
6. My Next Right Movement
One action for this week.
7. Soul Challenge
A practice to live the week, not just think it.
