Movement 2

My Truth

Look at each belief you carry about this part of your life. Is it true for me? Is it mine, or was it handed to me? Does it lift me, or weigh me down?

Beliefs people sometimes carry (which feel familiar?)

  • I have to earn my place.
  • Doubt means I'm doing it wrong.
  • My spiritual life is private, so I don't need anyone.
  • I'm too busy for this right now.
  • I'm held, even when I can't feel it.
  • There's a purpose in my being here.
